What this demonstrates

Scheduled flight, operator, route and calendar features combine with strictly lagged 28-day operating history. A LightGBM model estimates disruption risk; an isotonic mapping calibrated in May is evaluated in June. A separate quantile model estimates the 90th percentile of arrival delay for completed, non-diverted flights. A May-only conformal correction is frozen before June evaluation.

Brier score: 0.1995; 28-day route-history baseline: 0.2047. q90 coverage before/after correction: 81.8% / 87.1%; corrected MAE: 54.6 minutes.

Operations scenario

16242 plausible tail rotation links were reconstructed. A hypothetical buffer-allocation scenario is documented in the findings.

Interpretation

This is an analytical portfolio project using U.S. public data. The dataset has no weather forecasts, passenger itineraries, crews, gates, maintenance states or airline costs. The optimizer is a stated what-if exercise, not evidence of real-world savings or a dispatch recommendation.
