Method and reproducibility

Only fitting engines determine operating-regime clusters, normalization statistics, rolling-feature inputs and model coefficients. Validation engines choose among age baseline, Ridge and histogram gradient boosting. Calibration engines set interval width. Official test labels are used after selection and calibration for reporting, not training. No random row-level split or internal row-level early stopping is used.

Trailing sensor means, standard deviations and slopes use a 20-cycle window, resetting at engine boundaries. Training rows receive equal total weight per engine. Uncapped and clipped-target scores are saved separately. Errors overestimating remaining life incur a stronger asymmetric benchmark penalty; no financial maintenance savings are inferred.
